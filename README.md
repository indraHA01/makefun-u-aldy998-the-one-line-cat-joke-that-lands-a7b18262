# makefun-u-aldy998-the-one-line-cat-joke-that-lands-a7b18262

One line of cat comedy for make.fun's "The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands" bounty ($2, 1st place takes all).

## The entry

> My cat reviewed my cooking by trying to bury it like litter.

Why this angle: the crowded ground is the pun ("purr-fect", "cat-astrophe"), the box, the 3am zoomies, the knocked-off glass, and the aloof cat who ignores you — and the judge brief explicitly demotes puns heard many times before. This one goes where almost nobody goes: the litter box as a food critic. The setup is a domestic truth anyone who has cooked near a cat knows (the cat watches you cook), the turn is a real, specific cat behaviour (burying something disgusting), and the payoff lands in the last two words — the cat didn't dislike dinner, it classified it as waste. No pun, no explanation after the punchline, nothing to spare: the burying IS the review. Visual, retellable to a friend the same day, and it only works because it is about a cat — no other animal buries its food review in litter.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network, no randomness) writes the fixed joke to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking the house gate itself: 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one line.

```bash
node main.ts
```
